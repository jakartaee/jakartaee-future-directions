Meeting Notes – MicroProfile & Jakarta EE Working Group Merger Discussion

---

1. Purpose of Meeting

  • Discussion focused on whether MicroProfile and Jakarta EE working groups should merge and how to address long-standing concerns around fragmentation and duplication. 6:52

---

2. Key Themes & Discussion

Merger Rationale

  • Arjan highlighted historical separation due to Oracle-era constraints and argued that the ecosystem has naturally converged again, making a merge logical and reducing customer confusion. 10:29
  • Jan supported consolidation, noting community expectations for a single coherent platform and reduced duplication of specifications. 16:26
  • Tobias emphasized that merging working groups should be considered independently of namespace decisions. 26:09

Namespace Debate (secondary topic)

  • Emily stated that namespace changes are not technically required and should not block organizational decisions. 24:57
  • Arjan later agreed that namespace should not block progress and should be revisited after merger decisions. 55:23
  • Roberto warned that multiple namespace changes could introduce unnecessary maintenance overhead and fragmentation risk for implementations. 40:13

Community & Governance Concerns

  • Jan raised differences in governance models, voting rights, and membership structures between the two working groups as a key factor requiring clarity before merging. 54:04
  • Tobias highlighted cost considerations and sustainability of participating in multiple working groups. 27:08

Implementation & Ecosystem Impact

  • Roberto noted that implementations like SmallRye may reconsider or drop support if repeated renaming or restructuring increases maintenance burden. 41:32
  • He also emphasized that real-world usage already extends beyond strict MicroProfile APIs in many cases. 46:47

 

Convergence Perspective

  • Multiple participants agreed there is significant overlap in contributors, companies, and technical direction between both groups, effectively forming a shared community already. 36:43

---

3. Decisions / Outcomes

  • No formal decision was taken during the meeting.
  • General direction indicated growing support for:
  • Merging the working groups first
  • Deferring namespace decisions to a later stage
  • Consensus trend leaned toward prioritizing organizational merger over technical renaming concerns. 56:24

---

4. Key Takeaway

  • The primary unresolved tension remains sequencing: whether to merge first and decide namespace later, or tie both decisions together, with most participants leaning toward separating these concerns. 26:09
